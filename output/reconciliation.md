# Monthly reconciliation: usage to invoice to revenue to cash

Hedgehog Metrics, Inc. (synthetic data). Q1 2026. Generated by `python run.py`.

Each month has four bridges. Every difference is a named reconciling item with its SPEC reference. Each bridge totals its items, compares the total with an independent figure (the usage file, the invoices, the revenue schedule, or cash in the GL), and shows the unexplained difference, which must be zero.

Amounts in parentheses are deductions.
